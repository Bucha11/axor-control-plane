# Design Partner Program (3 slots) — internal 2-pager

**Who**: teams running ≥5 LLM agents in prod (or committed to, this quarter)
with a platform/SRE owner and a compliance or reliability pain. One partner
per vertical (fintech / healthcare / dev-tools) — reference diversity.

**They get**
- Team tier free for 12 months, then 50% off year 2 (cap: 30 nodes).
- Direct roadmap influence: their top-3 asks triaged into Next monthly.
- Private support channel, 48h SLA; migration help wiring the adapter.
- Co-authored public case study at graduation (their approval required).

**We get (the contract, in writing)**
- A named champion + 1h/biweekly working session.
- Production (or prod-shadow) traffic through the proxy within 30 days;
  adapter depth on ≥3 nodes within 90 days.
- Permission to cite: logo + one number (e.g. "caught N fabrications in
  month one") at graduation.
- Honest exit: either side can end it with 2 weeks notice; pricing reverts.

**Success criteria per partner (graduation = 2 of 3)**
1. An EvidenceCase from THEIR traffic shared internally beyond the champion.
2. Regression CI wired into their config-change flow (must-block + must-pass).
3. An operator intervention (pause/budget/stop) used in a real incident.

**Anti-goals**: no bespoke features that don't generalize; no free Enterprise
(SSO/RBAC stays paid); no unlimited slots — scarcity is the point.

**Pipeline**: paper reviewers' orgs · HN launch inbound · MCP-community teams.
Pitch line: "You get the receipts and the kill switch before your auditors
ask; we get proof it works outside our lab."

**Paperwork** (ready to send, see `partner/`): external one-pager ·
outreach templates + qualification checklist · Design Partner Agreement ·
mutual NDA · DPA template (dormant while self-hosted — see its "when this
applies").
