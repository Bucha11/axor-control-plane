# Axor Design Partner Program — 3 slots

**Your agent lies when its tools fail. We give you the receipt — and the kill
switch.**

Axor Control Plane is runtime governance for LLM agents: an observe-only
proxy in front of your agent's tools, fault injection to prove how the agent
behaves when things break, and a cryptographically signed **EvidenceCase** —
an audit-ready record of every caught fabrication. Plus live operator
control: pause, budget-cap, or stop an agent (and everything downstream of
it) mid-flight.

Self-hosted. Your traffic never leaves your infrastructure. Auth passes
through the proxy untouched; raw bodies are never stored. Apache-2.0 core.
