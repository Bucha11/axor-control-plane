# Mutual Non-Disclosure Agreement

*Plain-language template. The Design Partner Agreement already contains a
confidentiality clause (its §8) — use this standalone NDA only when a
prospect wants to talk details BEFORE signing the program agreement. Not
legal advice; see README.md.*

This Mutual Non-Disclosure Agreement ("NDA") is made on **[DATE]** between
**[VENDOR LEGAL NAME]** ("Axor") and **[COMPANY LEGAL NAME]** ("Company"),
together the "parties", to allow both sides to evaluate a potential design
partnership (the "**Purpose**").

## 1. Confidential Information

Non-public information disclosed by either party in connection with the
Purpose — in any form — that a reasonable person would treat as
confidential, including product plans, roadmaps, architecture, security
posture, incidents, metrics, and business terms.

It does NOT include information that: (a) is or becomes public through no
fault of the recipient; (b) the recipient already lawfully had; (c) the
recipient lawfully receives from a third party without duty of
confidentiality; (d) the recipient develops independently without using the
discloser's Confidential Information.

## 2. Obligations

The recipient will: (a) use Confidential Information only for the Purpose;
(b) protect it with at least reasonable care; (c) share it only with its
personnel and advisors who need it for the Purpose and are bound by
obligations at least as protective; (d) not reverse-engineer disclosed
materials except as allowed by their open-source licenses.

## 3. Compelled disclosure

Disclosure required by law, court, or regulator is permitted, with prompt
notice to the discloser where lawful so it can seek protection.

## 4. Term

This NDA covers disclosures made within **12 months** of the date above.
Obligations survive for **3 years** from each disclosure. On written
request, the recipient will delete or return Confidential Information,
except archival copies required by law or routine backups.

## 5. No license, no obligation

No license or IP right is granted by disclosure. Neither party is obliged
to proceed with any transaction. Neither party may announce the discussions
without the other's written consent.

## 6. General

Governing law **[JURISDICTION]**, venue **[CITY/COURT]**. Entire agreement
on this subject; changes in writing only; if a clause fails, the rest
stands. Money damages may be inadequate for a breach of this NDA — the
discloser may seek injunctive relief in addition to other remedies.
