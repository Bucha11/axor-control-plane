# Partner docs — what's here and how to use it

Everything needed to sign a design partner without outside counsel on day one.

| File | What it is | When it's used |
|---|---|---|
| `design-partner-onepager.md` | External 1-pager (the pitch) | Attach to outreach / send after the first call |
| `outreach-email.md` | Cold/warm outreach templates + follow-up | Sourcing the 3 slots |
| `design-partner-agreement.md` | The contract for the program itself | Signed once per partner, before onboarding |
| `mutual-nda.md` | Short mutual NDA | Before deep-dive calls if they ask; the DPA-A confidentiality clause covers the rest |
| `dpa.md` | Data Processing Agreement template | Only if we ever host/process their data — read its "when this applies" section first |

## How to execute

1. Fill every `[BRACKETED]` field (your legal name/entity, jurisdiction, dates).
2. Export to PDF (any markdown-to-PDF, or paste into Google Docs).
3. E-sign: free tiers of Dropbox Sign / PandaDoc / DocuSign all handle 3
   contracts a quarter. Both parties keep a countersigned copy.
4. Log the signed date + partner in a private tracker, next to the graduation
   criteria from `../design-partner-kit.md`.

## Honest disclaimer (read once)

These templates are drafted in plain language from common startup-contract
structure (Common Paper / Bonterms-class), **without a lawyer**. They are not
legal advice. They are deliberately short, mutual, and low-stakes — the
program is free software plus collaboration time, so the downside they must
cover is small. Two rules keep it that way:

- **Don't take money on these templates.** The moment a partner pays real
  invoices or an Enterprise deal appears, that contract gets professional
  review in the relevant jurisdiction. Budget for it then — one paid deal
  funds the review many times over.
- **Don't host their data on these templates.** Self-hosted keeps you out of
  data-processor territory (see `dpa.md`); a hosted offering is the other
  trigger for real counsel.

If a partner's legal team redlines the agreement — good news, they have
lawyers. Accept reasonable redlines; their review is your review.
