## 1. What this is

Axor develops the Axor Control Plane, self-hosted runtime-governance
software for LLM agents (the "**Software**"). Partner joins Axor's design
partner program (the "**Program**") to deploy the Software, use it on real
workloads, and shape its roadmap. This Agreement covers the Program terms;
the Software itself is licensed separately (Section 4).

## 2. What Axor provides

1. **Team tier at no charge** for 12 months from the Effective Date, then
   50% of list price for the following 12 months, for up to **30 governed
   nodes**.
2. **Roadmap input**: Partner's top-3 requests are reviewed and triaged into
   the public roadmap monthly. Triage is a good-faith commitment to
   consider, not a commitment to build.
3. **Support**: a private channel with a 48-hour response target on business
   days, and reasonable hands-on help integrating the Software.
4. No bespoke, Partner-only features. Everything built generalizes to the
   product.

## 3. What Partner provides

1. A named champion: **[NAME, ROLE]**, plus a 1-hour working session every
   two weeks.
2. Production or production-shadow traffic through the Software's proxy
   within 30 days of the Effective Date, and adapter-level integration on at
   least 3 agents within 90 days.
3. Honest feedback, including incidents where the Software failed or
   mis-fired.

## 4. Software license and data

1. The Software's open-source components are licensed under **Apache-2.0**;
   enterprise components under the source-available license in `ee/LICENSE`.
   This Agreement does not modify either license.
2. The Software is **self-hosted by Partner**. Axor does not receive, store,
   or process Partner's traffic, traces, or personal data under this
   Agreement, and the Software contains no phone-home telemetry. If the
   parties later adopt a hosted offering, they will execute a Data
   Processing Agreement first.

## 5. Publicity and case study

1. During the Program, neither party names the other publicly without
   written consent (email suffices).
2. At graduation (Section 7), Axor may reference Partner with (a) Partner's
   name/logo and (b) one metric agreed in writing — each specific use
   pre-approved by Partner, approval not unreasonably withheld.
3. The parties intend to co-author one public case study at graduation.
   Publication requires Partner's written approval of the final text.

## 6. Feedback and IP

1. Each party keeps everything it owned before and develops outside this
   Agreement.
2. Partner grants Axor a perpetual, irrevocable, royalty-free license to use
   feedback, suggestions, and bug reports without restriction or
   attribution. Feedback never includes Partner's data or Confidential
   Information.

## 7. Term, graduation, exit

1. Term: 12 months from the Effective Date, unless ended earlier.
2. **Either party may end the Program with 14 days' written notice, for any
   reason.** On exit, discounted pricing ends and Section 5 consents lapse
   except those already given in writing.
3. "Graduation" = at least two of: (a) an EvidenceCase from Partner traffic
   circulated beyond the champion; (b) regression CI wired into Partner's
   config-change flow; (c) an operator intervention used in a real incident.

## 8. Confidentiality

1. "Confidential Information" is non-public information disclosed by either
   party that a reasonable person would treat as confidential (roadmaps,
   incidents, metrics, architecture), excluding information that is public,
   already known, independently developed, or lawfully received elsewhere.
2. Each party uses the other's Confidential Information only for the
   Program, protects it with reasonable care, and discloses it only to
   people who need it and are bound by comparable obligations.
3. Compelled disclosure (law, court, regulator) is permitted with prompt
   notice where lawful.
4. These obligations last 3 years after the Agreement ends.

## 9. Warranties and liability

1. THE SOFTWARE AND THE PROGRAM ARE PROVIDED "AS IS", WITHOUT WARRANTIES OF
   ANY KIND, EXPRESS OR IMPLIED, INCLUDING MERCHANTABILITY, FITNESS FOR A
   PARTICULAR PURPOSE, AND NON-INFRINGEMENT. The Software observes and
   advises; Partner remains responsible for its agents and their actions.
2. Neither party is liable for indirect, incidental, consequential, or
   punitive damages, or lost profits/data.
3. Each party's total liability under this Agreement is capped at
   **USD 1,000** or the fees actually paid by Partner in the prior 12
   months, whichever is greater. The cap does not apply to breaches of
   Section 8 or to willful misconduct.
